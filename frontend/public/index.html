The treatment

How electrolysis works

A precise, science-backed process — and the only permanent hair removal method recognized by the FDA.

Products

Formulated for your skin

We use an antimicrobial pretreatment that balances the skin's pH and accelerates healing. Post-treatment care centers on an organically grown aloe gel blended with manuka honey, tea tree oil, and plant stem cells — to soothe, protect, and support collagen production.

The session

What to expect

Each appointment begins with a visual assessment of skin and hair. The skin is cleansed, then a fine probe is placed precisely into the follicle. A controlled energy pulse damages the root before the hair is gently removed. After the session, skin is cleansed again and an aftercare regimen is applied.

Care protocol

Skin treated as carefully as the hair

I

Pretreatment

A high-quality antimicrobial formula balances the skin's pH and creates an optimal environment for the procedure.

II

Precision removal

A fine probe is placed into each follicle individually. Energy is delivered only at the tip — protecting surrounding skin.

III

Post-care serum

A soothing serum quickly absorbed by skin fights infection, locks in moisture, and supports natural rejuvenation.

IV

Light therapy

A short light treatment targets acne-producing bacteria and promotes faster skin healing after the session.

V

Privacy by design

All windows are frosted. The suite is entirely private so you can comfortably treat hair anywhere on the body.

VI

Permanent results

Multiple sessions spaced correctly produce lifelong results. Most clients see significant clearing within a few months.

Important to know

Results are gradual — and permanent

Electrolysis is a process. Depending on hair density and growth cycles, a full clearance may take months or, for stronger hairs, over a year. Every session removes treated follicles permanently. The journey requires patience — the destination is lifelong freedom.

Technology

The finest tools in electrolysis

Equipment chosen not for cost but for what it delivers — faster sessions, better precision, and the most comfortable experience possible.

Primary machine

Apilus xCell

27.12 MHz

Highest frequency available

The Apilus xCell operates at the industry's highest frequency, which — combined with precise technique — delivers energy faster and more accurately than any alternative. Sessions can be shorter without sacrificing thoroughness. Longer sessions are more comfortable than with lower-frequency devices.

Specialized settings

Focused on the follicle

Synchro and blend settings allow the machine to concentrate energy precisely on the root and germ cells responsible for hair regrowth — without dispersing current through surrounding tissue. Skin integrity is actively protected during every pulse.

Probe technology

ISOGARD insulated probes

Skin-protective design

ISOGARD probes carry an insulating coating along the shaft, ensuring that electrical current only activates at the very tip — the point of the follicle. The outer layers of skin are shielded from any energy exposure throughout the entire treatment.

Maximum efficacy at the root

Because current is concentrated at the tip rather than dispersed along the probe's length, the energy delivered to the follicle's root cells is more potent and more precise. This translates to better clearance per session and a more thorough result over the course of treatment.

Sterilization

Medical-grade sanitation

All reusable instruments — including tweezers — pass through a two-stage sterilization process before every client. Single-use probes are never reused.

Stage 1

Ultrasonic bath

A medical-grade enzymatic solution in an ultrasonic cleaner breaks down and removes biological material before sterilization begins.

Stage 2

Autoclave

Instruments are individually sealed in sterile packaging and run through an autoclave — the same standard used in surgical environments.
